01 - About

From writing the code to building the team that writes it

I started as a Software Developer at ImageAPI in 2012, working on content management and media processing systems. Over eight years there I moved through Senior Software Developer and into Acting Technical Lead, picking up the habits - clear technical writing, honest estimates, mentoring by doing - that still shape how I manage.

Today I manage a team of eight across backend, cloud, and data. My job is roadmap and OKRs on one side, and 1:1s and career development on the other - with enough technical grounding to review architecture, unblock a stuck design, and know when a timeline is genuinely at risk.
